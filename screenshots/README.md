# Screenshots

Organised to mirror `aura-apps/screenshots`, adapted to Android's surfaces. Aura Android is phone-only, so the iOS-specific folders (watch, iPad, complications) have no counterpart here.

- `renders/` — offline renders of individual cards and full stacks, the way I use them to check layout without a device. Mirrors the iOS `renders/`.
- `play-phone/` — Play Store listing screenshots for the phone. The Google Play equivalent of the iOS `ASC-iphone/`.
- `proposals/` — work-in-progress mockups and design proposals. Mirrors the iOS `proposals/`.

Glance home-screen widgets (Layer G) will get their own `play-widgets/` folder once they exist.
