How the sky crop reframes the widget

The widget reuses the same baked hero art the phone draws, but a widget tile is short, so Jetpack Glance centre-crops the 4:3 art to fit. Left alone, that crop is mostly sky. Aura trims the top of the art before the crop by an amount that depends on the tile's shape. Each strip below is the real bundled art, cropped exactly as the widget does, at three trim strengths.

◑
A wide, short tile centre-crops to a thin band that is nearly all sky, so it trims the top 0.35 to bring the scene up. A tall tile keeps a taller crop that already shows the scene, so it trims nothing. The choice is made per tile from the live tile size (LocalSize, SizeMode.Exact), since Glance has no crop-alignment knob and the size is only known in the composition.
