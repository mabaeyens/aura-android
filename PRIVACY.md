# Privacy

Aura is a weather app. It is built to need as little of your data as possible, and it has no backend of its own: your phone talks directly to the public weather services and nothing passes through a server I run.

## What Aura collects about you

Nothing. Aura has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. I do not receive any information about you or your use of the app.

## What stays on your device

- Your saved locations and app settings are stored locally on your device.
- If you grant location permission, your approximate location is used on the device to find the nearest place and to compute sun times. Aura requests only coarse (approximate) location, only while the app is in use, and never in the background. Your location is not sent to me and is not sent to any service beyond what is needed to fetch the public forecast for that area.

## Who Aura talks to

To show the weather, Aura fetches public data directly from these services, the same way opening their websites would:

- AEMET OpenData, Spain's national weather agency, for forecasts, warnings, observations and UV.
- MITECO, for national air-quality data.
- Open-Meteo, for hourly UV data.
- Public RSS news feeds (such as RTVE and AEMET) for the news section.

These requests are subject to those providers' own privacy terms. Aura sends them only what a normal weather request needs (for example, the area you are viewing), and no personal identifiers.
